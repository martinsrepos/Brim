# Brim promo: beat sheet

Concept (picked): **one pill becomes everything.** The camera never leaves the black island on the
side-wing gradient. Every feature is the same element changing shape; it ends as the pill inside the
Brim app icon. No cards, no slide changes. Hard cuts only inside the notification burst and at the end card.

30 s, 30 fps. 16:9 1920x1080, 9:16 1080x1920, 1:1 1080x1080 from one timeline.

Rhythm: shots from 0.3 s (burst hits) to 2.2 s (holds); three rests (idle, panel, lock).
Sound: OneTake's synthesized palette in one room, fewer events than beats, no music.
